A little about us

Made with care.
Served with a smile.

We source fresh milk from villages around Courtallam, working alongside the local farmers whose dedication makes every batch possible. We believe great dairy begins with strong relationships and respect for the people behind every ingredient.

From that milk, we craft delightful dairy products and waffle treats with care. Our goal is simple: give customers something delicious to enjoy while helping local farmers and their families thrive.
